Survival Indicators

Survival on this island requires managing three critical bars visible at the bottom-left of the screen:

❤️ Health: Decreases when you drown, starve, freeze or consume raw food. Eating cooked meat restores it.
⚡ Energy: Drains by running, jumping, and swinging tools. It regenerates slowly when walking or standing still.
💧 Hydration: Decays over time. You must drink fresh water from the inland lake to restore it. Drinking salty sea water causes damage!
🎣 Fishing: Equip the Fishing Rod (Slot 6). Stand near deep water, look at the water, and Left Click to cast. Click again when you see a splash to catch fish.
SURVIVAL TIP: Always craft clothes. A straw hat or wooden boots significantly reduce hydration and energy decay!

Controls & Locomotion

Master the movement controls to explore the large volcanic island and navigate back to the sea:

⌨️ Movement: Use WASD or Arrow keys to walk around.
🏃 Sprinting: Hold the Shift key to run faster, but keep an eye on your Energy bar!
🤿 Swimming & Diving: Wade into water to swim automatically. Hold Space to swim up. Dive under to explore seabed corals!
⛵ Raft Navigation: Repair the boat to unlock ocean travel. You can steer rafts to explore other islands on the horizon.

Terraforming & Ore Extraction

The environment is fully destructible. Use your tools to shape the island or dig deep into the earth:

⛏️ Digging: Equip the Pickaxe (Slot 7) and Left Click on any voxel to excavate caves or carve tunnels.
🪨 Sculpting: Collect Sand or Stone blocks, select them in your hotbar, and place them to build bridges or repair holes.
🪙 Mining Gold Ore: Hit the glittering gold ore rocks with your Pickaxe to break them. Pick up the chunks to repair the boat!
🔥 Smelting: Build a Furnace. Insert wood or coal as fuel, along with raw ores (Copper, Silicon, Titanium) to smelt ingots and glass.
SURVIVAL TIP: Digging underground triggers transitions through layers. You will find light soil, clay, and granite deep down.

Crafting Station & Construction

Gather materials to craft tools, gear, survival structures, or bases:

🎒 Open Inventory: Press Tab or click the Backpack icon to view inventory and the Crafting Panel.
🌿 Basic Recipes: Craft Ropes from Leaves, Straw Hats for sun protection, and Campfires to cook raw food.
🏠 Base Building: Craft foundations, walls, doors, and roofs, and place them on the island to build customized shelters!
🌱 Farming: Craft a Planted Berry Bush using 3 Berries and place it. It will grow harvestable berries every 60 seconds.
🎋 Planting Canes: Craft a Planted Bamboo Cane using 2 Cane and 1 Rope, and place it. Hit it with your Spear or Axe to harvest cane.

Atmosphere, Seasons & Wildlife

The island is a living ecosystem with changing weather conditions, seasons, and local wildlife:

🌧️ Dynamic Weather: Experience rain, snow, heavy clouds, and tropical storms. Storms cause leaf roofs to slowly decay!
❄️ Seasons: Seasons affect temperature and weather. In Winter, the freshwater lake freezes completely, allowing you to walk on ice.
🦀 Hunting & Cooking: Hunt beach crabs or shallow-water fish using your Spear (Slot 1). Ignite campfires using wood to cook meat!
